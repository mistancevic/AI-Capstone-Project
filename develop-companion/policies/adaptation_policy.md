# Adaptation Policy (synthetic — the coach-approved math rules)

**A1 — Budget math.** Remaining budget for the meal being solved = daily
target − (planned meals already eaten + off-plan extras) − (upcoming fixed
commitments + remaining planned meals). Every number shown to the client
traces to the plan and this arithmetic. The agent never invents a number.

**A2 — Tolerance band.** A day is compliant when the projected day-end
total lands within ±150 kcal and ±10 g protein of target. The band is
data, not opinion; per-macro verdicts are independent.

**A3 — Ranking.** Protein fit is the anchor; calorie overshoot is
penalized harder than undershoot (undershoot can average out). Hard
filters first: restrictions and intolerances are never tradeable;
availability and prep time must fit the situation. Preferences and
dislikes affect ranking only.

**A4 — Never-skip bridge.** Every options card carries a bridge option
(shake, bar, deli protein) compatible with the client's restrictions, so
skipping is never the default answer.

**A5 — Imperfect day.** When no option can land in the band, return the
closest options labeled with their exact signed gap plus the multi-day
averaging strategy: calories average over a 3–7 day window; protein stays
the daily anchor. Math shortfalls NEVER escalate — only safety escalates.

**A6 — The confirm gate.** Nothing is logged and no state changes without
the client's explicit confirmation. Approving, editing a portion (math
recomputed live), or escalating is always the human's click.

**A7 — Plan authority.** The coach's plan is authoritative. The agent
adapts within it and never edits targets, meals, or rules. Target changes
are a coach conversation, declined and pointed to the coach.
